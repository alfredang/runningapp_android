# Privacy Policy — RunTrack GPS

_Last updated: 14 June 2026_

> **Canonical URL submitted to Google Play:** <https://www.tertiaryinfotech.com/privacy-policy-html>
> This Markdown is the fuller, app-specific version kept for reference.

RunTrack GPS ("the app") is developed by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us"). This
policy explains what the app accesses and how your data is handled. **In short: your data stays on
your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share it.**

## Information the app uses

- **Location (precise and approximate).** Used solely on your device to draw your running route and
  to calculate distance, pace, and time during a run. Location is accessed only while you are
  tracking a run. With background-location permission, tracking continues when the screen is off so
  your route is not interrupted. Your location is **never sent to us or any third party.**
- **Microphone / voice.** Used only for in-app voice commands ("start", "pause", "resume", "stop").
  Speech is processed by the Android speech recognizer on the device. The app does not record, store,
  or transmit audio.
- **Run history.** Completed runs (distance, time, pace, date) are stored **locally** on your device
  using Android's private app storage. They are not backed up to us.

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or any servers. The app has no network
backend. We have no access to your runs, location, or any personal information.

## Data sharing

None. No data is shared with third parties because no data leaves your device.

## Data retention and deletion

Run history remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove runs individually or use
**Clear** in the History screen, or uninstall the app to erase all stored data.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

## Permissions summary

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location (fine/coarse) | Track your route and measure distance/pace |
| Background location | Keep tracking while the screen is off during a run |
| Microphone | In-app voice commands |
| Notifications | Show the active "tracking your run" notification |
| Foreground service (location) | Keep GPS running reliably during a run |

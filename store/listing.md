# Play Store Listing — RunTrack GPS

## App name
RunTrack GPS

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Track your run by GPS — live route, distance, pace & voice feedback. All on-device.

## Full description (≤4000 chars)
RunTrack GPS is a clean, lightweight running tracker that does one thing well: helps you reach your
distance goal. Pick a preset (5, 10, 20, or 40 km) or a custom distance, press Start, and run — the
app draws your route live on a map and shows your distance, pace, and time in big, glanceable numbers.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. Your runs are
saved locally on your device and never uploaded.

FEATURES
• Distance goals — preset 5 / 10 / 20 / 40 km, or set any custom distance
• Real-time GPS tracking with smart noise filtering (ignores poor accuracy, jitter, and impossible jumps)
• Live route map with a follow-me camera and your start point marked
• Big, readable distance, pace, and time — easy to glance at mid-run
• Voice commands — just say “start”, “pause”, “resume”, or “stop”
• Spoken feedback — hear each kilometre, the halfway point, 90%, and your goal
• Background tracking — keeps recording with the screen locked or the phone in your pocket
• On-device run history — distance, time, pace, and date for every saved run
• Beautiful Material design with automatic light & dark mode

PERMISSIONS
• Location — to track your running route and measure distance (background location keeps tracking
  going while the screen is off).
• Microphone — only for in-app voice commands.
• Notifications — to show the “tracking your run” notification while a run is active.

RunTrack GPS is built for runners who want a focused, private, no-nonsense tracker. Lace up and go.
